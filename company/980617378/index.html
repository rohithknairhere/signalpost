THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
GAUPNE BILSENTER AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 95.310 (Reparasjon og vedlikehold av motorvogner), based in Luster, founded 1999-03-09. Registered purpose: Bilverksted, handel med biler og bilutstyr og dertil hørende virksomhet. Leadership: CEO Petter Skare and board chair Petter Skare. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 24.3 m and a net result of NOK 1.9 m. Revenue grew 3.0% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 10 full-time equivalents. It has 12 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Luster. Most recent dated activity: 2026-06-17 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-09-12 Registered roles updated (REVI).
