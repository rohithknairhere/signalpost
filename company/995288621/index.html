THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BMO TUNNELSIKRING AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.990 (Annen spesialisert bygge- og anleggsvirksomhet ikke nevnt annet sted), based in Bergen, founded 2010-02-22. Registered purpose: Entreprenørvirksomhet innen vedlikehold, sikring og rehabilitering av tunneler for vei og bane, samt annen ras- og fjellsikring og annet anleggsarbeid og alt som står i forbindelse med dette. Leadership: CEO Vidar Pettersen and board chair Mikael André Kro. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 165.9 m and a net result of NOK -19.5 m. Revenue fell 35.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 43 full-time equivalents. It has 35 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Bergen. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-05 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2026-02-26 Registered roles updated (DAGL); 2026-02-26 Registered roles updated (STYR).
