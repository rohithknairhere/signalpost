THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BARNEKREFTFORENINGEN is registered as forening/lag/innretning (FLI) under industry code 94.992 (Aktiviteter i andre medlemsorganisasjoner ellers), based in Oslo, founded 1982-09-26. Its website describes it as: “Vi er en frivillig, landsdekkende organisasjon av og for foreldre som har eller har hatt barn med kreft. 4 av 5 barn overlever barnekreft, vi vil redde alle!” Leadership: CEO Trine Beate Wessel Nicolaysen and board chair Geir Brekke Jenssen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 127.4 m and a net result of NOK 51.9 m. Revenue fell 73.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It has 25 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Its verified website is https://www.barnekreftforeningen.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram, Linkedin, Tiktok. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-15 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2026-01-22 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed).
