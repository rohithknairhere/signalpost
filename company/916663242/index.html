THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
KRONBORG TECHNOLOGIES AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 26.110 (Produksjon av elektroniske komponenter), based in Horten, founded 2016-01-04. Registered purpose: Utvikling, produksjon og salg av elektronisk utstyr til offshore og landbaserte virksomheter, herunder handel med tilsvarende produkter for salg. Leadership: CEO Willy Kronborg and board chair Terje Midtskogen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 319 k and a net result of NOK -439 k. Revenue grew 124.8% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Horten. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-12-06 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2020-03-30 Share capital registered as 915490.0 NOK.
