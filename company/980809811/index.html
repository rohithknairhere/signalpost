THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
FIX SKOREPARASJON AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 95.230 (Reparasjon og vedlikehold av skotøy og lærvarer), based in Moss, founded 1999-05-12. Registered purpose: Skoreparasjon, herunder salg av varer i tilknytning til dette samt investering i aksjer mm, og det som naturlig faller inn under dette. Leadership: CEO Kai Kvennsjø and board chair Kai Kvennsjø. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 5.9 m and a net result of NOK 1.1 m. Revenue fell 11.0% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 4 full-time equivalents. It has 5 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Moss. Most recent dated activity: 2026-04-08 Articles of association dated; 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2008-11-03 Registered roles updated (REGN).
