THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
SUCCEDO AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 78.200 (Utleie av arbeidskraft og andre personaladministrative tjenester), based in Oslo, founded 2014-03-25. Its website describes it as: “Hos Succedo møter du erfarne rådgivere med solid forretningsforståelse, bred sektorkompetanse og omfattende nettverk. Les mer her.” It presents itself publicly as “Succedo – Executive search | Strategisk rådgivning | Management for hire”. Leadership: CEO Morten Dreyer and board chair Carl Otto Henrik Renman. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 38.4 m and a net result of NOK 2.7 m. Revenue grew 25.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 8 full-time equivalents. It has 16 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Its verified website is https://www.succedo.no/. Its website has a careers page, but no active NAV postings were found. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-03-03 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2022-01-03 Registered roles updated (REVI).
