THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
FLUIDO NORWAY AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 62.100 (Dataprogrammeringstjenester), based in Oslo, founded 2015-05-22. Registered purpose: IT- og forretningsrådgivning, opplæring, programvare og utvikling av programvare, videresalg av forretningsapplikasjoner. Leadership: board chair Thomas Mark Mikael Johanson. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 72.4 m and a net result of NOK 10.3 m. Revenue fell 11.2% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 19 full-time equivalents. It has 21 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-06 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2021-11-30 Registered roles updated (STYR).
