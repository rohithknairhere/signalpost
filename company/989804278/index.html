THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
FRILUFTSBUTIKKEN AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 47.631 (Detaljhandel med sportsvarer), based in Tromsø, founded 2006-04-07. Its website describes it as: “Friluftsbutikken | Karlsøyvegen 1 A | 9015 Tromsø | | Epost: post@friluftsbutikken.no | Tlf: 77615304” Leadership: CEO Stein Otto Ingebrigtsen and board chair Stein Otto Ingebrigtsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 11.4 m and a net result of NOK 115 k. Revenue grew 9.2% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 3.3 full-time equivalents. It has 7 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Tromsø. Its verified website is https://friluftsbutikken.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-14 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2015-10-30 Registered roles updated (REVI).
