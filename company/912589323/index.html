THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BYGGMESTER JOSTEIN GARNES AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 41.000 (Oppføring av bygninger), based in Bergen, founded 2013-10-01. Registered purpose: Håndverk, handel og tjenesteyting, og hva dermed står i forbindelse, herunder ved innskudd av kapital, eventuelt ved aksjetegning å delta i andre virksomheter i inn og utland. Leadership: CEO Jan Gustav Garnes and board chair Tormod Raa. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 39.3 m and a net result of NOK 1.7 m. Revenue fell 6.5% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 16 full-time equivalents. It has 23 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Bergen. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-31 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2026-10-06 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed).
