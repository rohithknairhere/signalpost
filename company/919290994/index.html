THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
KOLO AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.330 (Gulvlegging og tapetsering), based in Moss, founded 2017-05-08. Its website describes it as: “Vi er et ungt selskap med fire ansatte som jobber med både interiør, oppussing og renovering. Vi tar hvert prosjekt på alvor og med iver” Leadership: CEO Lukasz Pawel Koloda and board chair Lukasz Pawel Koloda. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 5.4 m and a net result of NOK -29 k. Revenue fell 15.0% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 4 full-time equivalents. It has 6 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Moss. Its verified website is https://koloas.no/. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2018-05-24 Changed name from FOKHUS HÅNDVERK AS; 2018-04-26 Articles of association dated.
