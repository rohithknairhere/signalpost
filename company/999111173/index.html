THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
RØDMYRLIA 39 AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 68.200 (Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom), based in Notodden, founded 2012-07-10. Registered purpose: Eie og forvalte aksjer, verdipapirer, fast eiendom og andre formuesverdier av hvilken som helst art. Leadership: board chair Arve Bakken. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 7.9 m and a net result of NOK 3.6 m. Revenue grew 8.1% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Notodden.
