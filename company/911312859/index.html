THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BRØDRENE EIDSAND AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.120 (Grunnarbeid), based in Gran, founded 1968-05-21. Registered purpose: Maskinentrepenørvirksomhet. Leadership: CEO Jarle Eidsand and board chair Bjørn Eidsand. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 51.2 m and a net result of NOK 2.7 m. Revenue grew 2.3% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 18 full-time equivalents. It has 31 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Gran. Most recent dated activity: 2026-01-16 Share capital registered as 366000.0 NOK; 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-12-15 Articles of association dated.
