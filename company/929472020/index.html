THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
VINJES TRANSPORT AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.990 (Annen spesialisert bygge- og anleggsvirksomhet ikke nevnt annet sted), based in Trondheim, founded 1980-12-01. Registered purpose: Transportvirksomhet, verksted og skadebegrensning, samt annen virksomhet som står i forbindelse med dette herunder å delta i andre selskaper ved aksje- besittelse eller på annen måte. Leadership: CEO Erik Vinje and board chair Harald Kuvås. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 201.8 m and a net result of NOK 4.1 m. Revenue fell 1.2% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). It has 151 registered employees. It operates 3 registered workplaces in Trondheim. Customers rate it 4.6/5 from 114 reviews (Google reviews (as shown by Fagfolkguiden)). Most recent dated activity: 2026-02-07 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2021-08-23 Registered roles updated (REVI).
