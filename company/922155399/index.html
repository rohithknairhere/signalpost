THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
SABRURA STEINKJER AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 56.110 (Drift av restauranter), based in Steinkjer, founded 2019-01-08. Registered purpose: Cafe- og serveringsvirksomhet, og dermed alt som står i den forbindelse. Leadership: CEO Malin Evjen and board chair Malin Evjen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 13.6 m and a net result of NOK 502 k. Revenue grew 7.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 6 full-time equivalents. It has 20 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Steinkjer. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2021-07-14 Registered roles updated (DAGL); 2021-04-08 Registered roles updated (STYR).
