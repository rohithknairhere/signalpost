THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
DRENGENES TAK OG BLIKK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.410 (Takarbeid), based in Bergen, founded 2018-08-24. Registered purpose: Selge tjenester innen bygg og anlegg og annet som naturlig sammenfaller med dette, samt deltakelse i andre selskaper, investeringer og nærliggende virksomhet. Leadership: CEO Jørn Ove Drengenes and board chair Jørn Ove Drengenes. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 23.3 m and a net result of NOK 14 k. Revenue grew 14.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 9 full-time equivalents. It has 15 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Bergen. Most recent dated activity: 2026-02-05 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2019-11-13 Registered roles updated (REVI).
