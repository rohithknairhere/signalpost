THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
MAVI LOGISTIKK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 49.410 (Godstransport på vei), based in Oslo, founded 2013-08-10. Its website describes it as: “Spesialister på hjemlevering med tilleggstjenester” Leadership: CEO Tommy Larsen and board chair Cristian Mazzola. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 64.7 m and a net result of NOK 3.5 m. Revenue grew 11.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 13 full-time equivalents. It has 16 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Its verified website is https://www.mavilogistikk.no/. Most recent dated activity: 2026-02-03 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-10-15 Registered roles updated (REVI).
