THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
AQUAPREDICT AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 71.129 (Annen teknisk konsulentvirksomhet), based in Bodø, founded 2023-03-28. Registered purpose: Utvikling og salg av teknologi for bruk i akvakultur og det som naturlig står i forbindelse med det. Leadership: CEO Kjetil Korsnes and board chair Oddmund Edvard Åsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 1.1 m and a net result of NOK -761 k. Revenue fell 1.9% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 2.93 full-time equivalents. It has 6 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Bodø. Most recent dated activity: 2026-08-26 Share capital registered as 123960.0 NOK; 2026-01-09 Articles of association dated; 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed).
