THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
STADION 33 AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 68.200 (Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom), based in Oslo, founded 2007-06-25. Registered purpose: Handel med og investering i fast eiendom, verdipapirer og andre formuesobjekter, herunder deltakelse i andre selskap med liknende virksomhet. Leadership: board chair Torjus Mykland. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 10.3 m and a net result of NOK 4.7 m. Revenue grew 2.4% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-03-04 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2024-12-12 Changed name from BRUNO AS.
