THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
RB PROFF PARTNER AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 41.000 (Oppføring av bygninger), based in Oslo, founded 2020-02-01. Registered purpose: Byggetjenester, rehabilitering, flislegging, gulvlegging, maling inn- og utvendig, snekring, sparkling, renhold, samt andre tjenester som naturlig hører sammen med dette. It presents itself publicly as “Din profesjonelle byggepartner”. Leadership: CEO Robert Bazydlo and board chair Robert Bazydlo. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 5.3 m and a net result of NOK -433 k. Revenue fell 6.0% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 8 full-time equivalents. It has 8 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Its verified website is https://www.rbproffpartner.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-11-02 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2020-02-11 Registered in Enhetsregisteret.
