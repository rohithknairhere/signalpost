THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
TUSENFRYD BARNEHAGE STRANDA SA is registered as samvirkeforetak (SA) under industry code 85.100 (Førskoleundervisning og drift av barnehager), based in Stranda, founded 1995-03-28. Registered purpose: Eie og drive barnehage til det beste for medlemmene og deres barn. Foretaket skal fremme medlemmenes økonomiske interesser gjennom deres deltakelse i virksomheten som kjøpere av barnehagetjenester fra foretaket. Leadership: CEO Gro Anita Rjånes and board chair Live Slettvoll Myklebust. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 12.9 m and a net result of NOK 805 k. Revenue grew 9.1% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 17 full-time equivalents. It has 23 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Stranda. Most recent dated activity: 2026-01-12 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2012-11-12 Changed name from TUSENFRYD BARNEHAGE LL.
