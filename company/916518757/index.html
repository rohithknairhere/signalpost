THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BABIS PIZZA AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 56.110 (Drift av restauranter), based in Nittedal, founded 2015-12-17. Its website describes it as: “Babis Pizza er Nittedals eldste og beste pizzeria. Hjemmelaget bunn hver eneste dag.” It presents itself publicly as “Babispizza”. Leadership: CEO Charalampos Karampakalis and board chair Charalampos Karampakalis. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 949 k and a net result of NOK -580 k. Revenue fell 74.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 3 full-time equivalents. It has 9 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Nittedal. Its verified website is https://www.babispizza.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-07-10 Articles of association dated; 2016-01-06 Registered in Enhetsregisteret.
