THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
NAUSTDAL BLIKK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 43.223 (Ventilasjonsarbeid), based in Sunnfjord, founded 2023-02-05. Registered purpose: Salg og produksjon av blikkenslagerprodukt eller virksomheit som har tilknytning til dette, herunder ved innskot av kapital, eventuelt ved aksjeteikning å delta i andre verksemnder. Samt utleige av fast eigendom. Leadership: CEO Ole Martin Einen Larsen and board chair Ole Martin Einen Larsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 6.1 m and a net result of NOK 66 k. Revenue fell 44.8% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 4 full-time equivalents. It has 5 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Sunnfjord. Customers rate it 4.2/5 from 5 reviews (Google reviews (as shown by Fagfolkguiden)). Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-06-18 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2023-03-02 Registered in Enhetsregisteret.
