THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
MELINGS-OSV. AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 68.200 (Utleie av egen eller leid fast eiendom), based in Stavanger, founded 2020-04-01. Registered purpose: Utleie av eiendommer, samt alt hva hermed står i forbindelse, herunder deltakelse i selskap med lignende virksomhet. Leadership: CEO Svein Olav Sivertsen and board chair Svein Olav Sivertsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 2.6 m and a net result of NOK 13 k. Revenue grew 1.5% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Stavanger. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-09-13 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2023-10-25 Registered roles updated (STYR).
