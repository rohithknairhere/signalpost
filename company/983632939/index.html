THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
DOMBÅS BIL AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 95.310 (Reparasjon og vedlikehold av motorvogner), based in Dovre, founded 2001-08-14. Its website describes it as: “Ditt lokale bilverksted med over 30 års erfaringVi tilbyr et bredt spekter av verkstedtjenester for lette kjøretøy opp til 3,5 tonn. Dette inkluderer PKK,” Leadership: CEO Inge Birger Groven and board chair Knut Terje Svelstad. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 16.9 m and a net result of NOK -380 k. Revenue grew 10.4% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 9.9 full-time equivalents. It has 18 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Dovre. Its verified website is https://dombasbil.no/. Customers rate it 5.0/5 from 22 reviews (Google reviews (as shown by Fagfolkguiden)). Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-10-14 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2021-12-15 Registered roles updated (REGN).
