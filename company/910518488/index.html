THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
Bachke & Co. A/S is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 52.211 (Drift av gods- og transportsentraler), based in Trondheim, founded 1977-09-22. Its website describes it as: “Bachke & Co. A/S har følgende virksomheter: Tekniske tjenester, regnskapsførertjenester og terminaldrift for kunder i inn- og utland” Leadership: CEO Frode Arnesen and board chair Paul Emil Hjelm-Hansen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 33.5 m and a net result of NOK 4.6 m. Revenue fell 20.9% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 18 full-time equivalents. It has 20 registered employees. It operates 3 registered workplaces in Hitra, Trondheim. Its verified website is https://www.bachke.no/. Customers rate it 5.0/5 from 1 reviews (Google reviews (as shown by Fagfolkguiden)). Most recent dated activity: 2026-08-05 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-11-14 Articles of association dated.
