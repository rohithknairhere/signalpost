THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
LÆRINGSVERKSTEDET KROKUSBAKKEN BARNEHAGE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 85.100 (Førskoleundervisning og drift av barnehager), based in Ullensaker, founded 2022-11-25. Registered purpose: Eie og drift av barnehager samt rådgivning og andre oppgaver tilknyttet dette. Leadership: board chair Trude Moen Sydtangen. Its filed 2025 accounts show a net result of NOK -1.1 m. Revenue grew 5.5% from 2023 to 2024 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 27 full-time equivalents. It has 33 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Bergen. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-01-10 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2023-03-03 Registered roles updated (REGN).
