THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
VINTAGE CAPITAL AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 68.320 (Eiendomsforvaltning på oppdrag), based in Oslo, founded 1963-12-19. Registered purpose: Handel og konsulentvirksomhet, eiendomsfor- valtning, investering i fast eiendom, samt aksjer og verdipapirer, herunder også deltagelse i andre selskaper. Leadership: CEO Christian Fredrik Furuholmen and board chair Øystein Bøe. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 27.0 m and a net result of NOK 23.6 m. Revenue fell 57.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-06 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-11-16 Registered roles updated (REGN).
