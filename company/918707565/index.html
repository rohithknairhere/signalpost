THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BERTEL O. STEEN VESTFOLD OG TELEMARK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 95.310 (Reparasjon og vedlikehold av motorvogner), based in Tønsberg, founded 1967-11-10. Registered purpose: Handel og drift av bilverksted og tilknyttet virksomhet. Leadership: CEO Joachim Opthun Cedergren and board chair Per-Kristian Wendt Jonassen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 963.1 m and a net result of NOK -3.4 m. Revenue grew 8.2% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). It has 143 registered employees. It operates 5 registered workplaces in Larvik, Skien, Tønsberg. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-11-18 Registered roles updated (DAGL); 2023-11-29 Changed name from BERTEL O STEEN VESTFOLD AS.
