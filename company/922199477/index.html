THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
DIFFER NORGE HOLDING AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 70.200 (Bedriftsrådgivning og annen administrativ rådgivning), based in Oslo, founded 2019-01-25. Registered purpose: Selv, eller gjennom hel- eller deleide selskaper, å drive virksomhet innenfor strategi, organisasjonsutvikling og omstilling, management consulting, utleie av personell samt annen dermed forenlig virksomhet. Leadership: CEO Svein Arne Lamo and board chair Lina Johanna Hed. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 11.0 m and a net result of NOK 4.4 m. Revenue grew 15.1% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-05 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2026-07-08 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2026-04-25 Registered roles updated (DAGL).
