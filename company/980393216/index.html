THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
MULTIOPTIKK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 46.430 (Engroshandel med elektriske husholdningsapparater), based in Oslo, founded 1998-11-01. Its website describes it as: “MultiOptikk leverer alle typer forstørrende hjelpemidler og spesialoptiske løsninger for arbeid, fritid og hobby.” Leadership: CEO Anne Engebretsen and board chair Anne Engebretsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 20.8 m and a net result of NOK 299 k. Revenue grew 0.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 8.5 full-time equivalents. It has 8 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Oslo. Its verified website is https://multioptikk.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook.
