THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
WYSSEN NORGE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 71.129 (Annen teknisk konsulentvirksomhet), based in Sogndal, founded 2015-10-30. Registered purpose: (A) Produksjon, reparasjon, kjøp, salg, forsendelser, leasing, distribusjon, import, eksport og representasjon for følgende utstyr, reservedeler og tilbehør: (i) Utstyr for snøskredkontroll og andre naturfarer, inkludert men ikke begrenset Leadership: CEO Stian Langeland and board chair Christian Wyssen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 39.2 m and a net result of NOK 3.4 m. Revenue grew 107.4% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 6 full-time equivalents. It has 5 registered employees. It operates 2 registered workplaces in Sogndal, Tromsø. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-09-16 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2023-12-16 Registered roles updated (REGN).
