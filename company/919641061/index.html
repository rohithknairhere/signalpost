THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
EB REGNSKAP AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 69.202 (Regnskapsføring og bokføring), based in Smøla, founded 2017-08-05. Its website describes it as: “Betten Regnskap holder til på Hopen i Smøla Kommune. Vi jobber alltid for at våre kunder skal bli fornøyd og yter det lille ekstra for våre kunder. Vi har 9 dyktige medarbeidere med lang erfaring. -Velkommen til oss! ​” It presents itself publicly as “Bettenregnskap”. Leadership: CEO Egil Kåre Betten and board chair Egil Kåre Betten. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 16.5 m and a net result of NOK 5.1 m. Revenue grew 10.9% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 11 full-time equivalents. It has 13 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Smøla. Its verified website is https://www.bettenregnskap.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2019-09-21 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2017-10-13 Registered in Enhetsregisteret.
