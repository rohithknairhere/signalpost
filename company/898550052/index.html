THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
BJERKÅSHOLMEN BARNEHAGE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 85.100 (Førskoleundervisning og drift av barnehager), based in Asker, founded 2012-05-15. Registered purpose: Drift av barnehage. Leadership: CEO Ksenia Asanovna Engelschiøn and board chair Jan-Ståle Åsvang. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 9.2 m and a net result of NOK 31 k. Revenue grew 11.0% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 7.25 full-time equivalents. It has 11 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Asker. Its verified website is https://bjerkasholmenbarnehage.no/. Its website has a careers page, but no active NAV postings were found. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-06-19 Changed name from VOLLEN KYSTKULTURBARNEHAGE AS; 2025-05-08 Articles of association dated.
