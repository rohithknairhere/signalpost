THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
AIOTA AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 62.100 (Dataprogrammeringstjenester), based in Kristiansund, founded 2024-06-15. Its website describes it as: “Ett system for full kontroll på det bedriften eier, lagrer, bygger og vedlikeholder.” It presents itself publicly as “Lager, utstyr, prosjekt og vedlikehold”. Leadership: CEO Mikael Lervik Thorsgaard and board chair Mikael Lervik Thorsgaard. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 658 k and a net result of NOK -281 k. Revenue fell 28.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Kristiansund. Its verified website is https://aiota.no/. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-07-12 Registered in Enhetsregisteret; 2024-07-12 Share capital registered as 30000.0 NOK.
