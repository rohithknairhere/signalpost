THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
STEINKJER DYREKLINIKK AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 75.000 (Veterinærtjenester), based in Steinkjer, founded 2010-09-13. Registered purpose: Veterinærvirksomhet samt det som naturlig står i forbindelse hermed. Leadership: CEO Inga Rapp Friedl and board chair Vegard Forbord. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 7.0 m and a net result of NOK 139 k. Revenue grew 5.4% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 5 full-time equivalents. It has 8 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Steinkjer. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-12-11 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2020-08-27 Registered roles updated (DAGL).
