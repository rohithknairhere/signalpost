THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
STRAUME TEKNISKE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 71.129 (Annen teknisk konsulentvirksomhet), based in Øygarden, founded 2012-08-01. Its website describes it as: “Straume Tekniske, startet i 2012, tilbyr skreddersydde løsninger i ventilasjon, varmepumper og byggautomasjon for å optimalisere energiforbruket.” Leadership: CEO Kristian Gihl Tøgard and board chair Dag-Helge Bernhardsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 37.8 m and a net result of NOK 1.0 m. Revenue fell 2.5% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 16 full-time equivalents. It has 23 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Øygarden. Its verified website is https://www.straumetekniske.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram, Linkedin. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-08-04 Registered roles updated (DAGL); 2024-12-04 Registered roles updated (REVI).
