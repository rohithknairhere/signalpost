THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
OPSIS AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 74.110 (Industri-, produkt- og motedesignvirksomhet), based in Trysil, founded 2011-11-11. Its website describes it as: “Opsis er en kreativ print- og profileringsbedrift med produksjonslokaler og kontor i Trysil. Med vår brede kompetanse og iveren etter å...” Leadership: CEO Steinar Larsen Eriksson and board chair Anders Nyhuus. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 8.7 m and a net result of NOK -170 k. Revenue grew 14.5% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 3 full-time equivalents. It has 6 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Trysil. Its verified website is https://www.opsis.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram. Most recent dated activity: 2026-04-21 Registered roles updated (REGN); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-09-26 Registered roles updated (REVI).
