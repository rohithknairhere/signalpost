THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
INTERSPORT RONGVE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 47.631 (Detaljhandel med sportsvarer), based in Ålesund, founded 2022-04-11. Registered purpose: Drive handel med sportsartikler og -utstyr og det som naturlig står iforbindelse med dette. Leadership: CEO Viktor Lillebø Rongve and board chair Terje Woldseth. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 18.6 m and a net result of NOK -1000 k. Revenue fell 7.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 6 full-time equivalents. It has 22 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Ålesund.
