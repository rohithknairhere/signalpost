THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
ARKITEKTFIRMA JON VIKØREN AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 71.110 (Arkitektvirksomhet), based in Vik, founded 2003-02-05. Registered purpose: Yta rådgjevande arkitektfaglege tenester. Selskapet kan også gjennom aksjekjøp eller på andre måtar engasjera seg i andre føretak uansett føremål og selskapsform. Leadership: CEO Lin Marie Vikøren and board chair Lin Marie Vikøren. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 4.2 m and a net result of NOK 5 k. Revenue fell 10.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 3 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Vik. Most recent dated activity: 2026-08-19 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2004-08-19 Registered roles updated (DAGL).
