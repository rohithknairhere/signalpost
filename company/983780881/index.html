THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
SLÅTTSVEEN TRANSPORT AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 49.410 (Godstransport på vei), based in Østre Toten, founded 2001-09-01. Registered purpose: Godstransport med lastebil, samt andre typer aktiviteter som kan bidra til å fremme hovedformålet. Leadership: CEO John Slåttsveen Enerberg and board chair Terje Slåttsveen Enerberg. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 19.4 m and a net result of NOK 710 k. Revenue fell 1.3% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 8 full-time equivalents. It has 10 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Østre Toten. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2022-01-07 Registered roles updated (DAGL); 2022-01-07 Registered roles updated (STYR).
