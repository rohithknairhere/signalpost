THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
INSBO SIRNES FARGE & INTERIØR AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 47.520 (Detaljhandel med jernvarer, byggevarer, fargevarer og glass), based in Flekkefjord, founded 1991-04-30. Registered purpose: Handel og agenturvirksomhet og hva dermed står i forbindelse. Selskapet kan delta i andre selskaper med liknende formål. Revenue grew 15.6% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 0 full-time equivalents. It has 9 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Flekkefjord.
