THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
MIDT-BYGG AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 41.000 (Oppføring av bygninger), based in Trondheim, founded 2015-08-05. Registered purpose: Byggevirksomhet og tilhørende aktiviteter, heunder deltakelse i andre selskap, samt det som naturlig står i forbindelse med dette. Leadership: CEO Martin Arntsen Johnsen and board chair Martin Arntsen Johnsen. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 23.0 m and a net result of NOK 84 k. Revenue grew 10.1% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 10.17 full-time equivalents. It has 16 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Trondheim. Its verified website is https://www.midtbygg.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram. Customers rate it 2.4/5 from 9 reviews (Google reviews (as shown by Fagfolkguiden)). Most recent dated activity: 2026-10-06 Registered roles updated (REVI); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2024-03-25 Registered roles updated (STYR).
