THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
WIIG GARTNERI AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 01.130 (Dyrking av grønnsaker, meloner, rot- og knollvekster), based in Klepp, founded 1998-12-22. Its website describes it as: “Wiig Gartneri er et av de største gartnerier i Norge og holder til på Orre på Jæren og dyrker blant annet Piccolotomater, Perletomater, Mikstomater og agurker. I sommerhalvåret har vi åpent gartneriutsalg hver dag.” Leadership: CEO Anne Siril Wiig and board chair Anne Siril Wiig. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 179.5 m and a net result of NOK 11.5 m. Revenue grew 8.3% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 86.16 full-time equivalents. It has 90 registered employees. It operates 1 registered workplace in Klepp. Its verified website is https://www.wiig-gartneri.no/ and it links to company profiles on Facebook, Instagram. Most recent dated activity: 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2025-03-07 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2020-12-03 Registered roles updated (REVI).
