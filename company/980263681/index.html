THE EVIDENCE BRIEF
ÅSGREINA BYGGASSISTANSE AS is registered as aksjeselskap (AS) under industry code 41.000 (Oppføring av bygninger), based in Gjerdrum, founded 1998-10-26. Registered purpose: Bygg- og entreprenørvirksomhet samt vedlikehold og annen virksomhet som står i forbindelse med dette samt deltagelse i andre selskaper. Leadership: CEO Leander Slåttum Vesterås and board chair Oliver Slåttum Vesterås. Its filed 2025 accounts show revenue of NOK 4.4 m and a net result of NOK 206 k. Revenue grew 49.7% from 2024 to 2025 (computed from filed values). Its 2025 annual report states 3 full-time equivalents. It operates 1 registered workplace in Gjerdrum. Most recent dated activity: 2026-02-14 Registered roles updated (STYR); 2025-12-31 Financial year 2025 closed (accounts filed); 2021-10-28 Registered roles updated (DAGL).
